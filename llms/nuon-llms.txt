# Nuon documentation

> Nuon is the BYOC (bring-your-own-cloud) deployment platform for software vendors. Deploy applications — Terraform, Helm, Kubernetes manifests, container images — into customer AWS, Azure, or GCP accounts with no cross-account IAM. Includes runners, operation roles for least-privilege IAM, drift detection, approval workflows, and policies for compliance.
